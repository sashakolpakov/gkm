{\Large\bfseries Response to comments}\par
Alexander Kolpakov

The revision centres conditional marginal complexity and its evidence in the retained verified solver history. Locations refer to the revised manuscript.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Central question and mathematical background $\rightarrow$} The abstract, Introduction, Methods and Discussion now centre the additional description needed for the next verified solver, given the retained program. Standard categorical and structure-function results are identified as background and their exposition shortened.
\item \textbf{Marginal complexity and source growth $\rightarrow$} Methods distinguish ideal conditional Kolmogorov complexity from the fixed compressed-AST novelty proxy M and the line/literal counters C and G. The source-growth counters are supporting accounting, not substitutes for conditional complexity or measures of complete discovery cost.
\item \textbf{Marginal profiles and sawtooth interpretation $\rightarrow$} The main figure plots conditional novelty for dc22, wa30 and ls20; the full-suite appendix publishes all 25 games, leaving unknown boundaries as gaps. The generalized code-only accountant follows calls through new glue across all 144 GKM source pairs, finding retained paths in 115, including 18 sharp marginal drops. A case audit documents both dc22 dips and both wa30 troughs. It also identifies cn04 L3 as a smaller literal action player. The Results and Discussion use these concrete source findings to explain the different dips.
\item \textbf{Candidate selection $\rightarrow$} The architecture description specifies first success at the exact next boundary under execution, independent replay, taint/provenance and containment checks. Complexity and free energy are diagnostics after candidate fixation; ranking remains proposed. Older acquisition controls and later endpoint certification are distinguished.
\item \textbf{dc22 accounting and source provenance $\rightarrow$} The full recorded charge remains 650. On authenticated L2--L6, C sums to 601 and G to 801 as retained size grows from 50 to 851. Complete winning source corrects the manuscript interpretation and audit reconstruction without repairing the valid solver or counter. Conditional novelty falls from 885 to 828 bytes at L5: a modest dip, despite increasing source growth, rather than the previously reported sharp contraction.
\item \textbf{Exact sources and static calls $\rightarrow$} The audit admits 171 exact winning sources, 144 adjacent transitions and 141 marginal comparisons, without bridging missing sources. It finds 64 decreases, 21 half-or-more drops, 54 static unchanged-leg call-site cases and 13 sharp coupled cases. Those fixed direct-call statistics remain for comparison. Generalized source paths and independently observed replay calls now have separate fields; the historical campaign has no retrospective runtime-call observations.
\item \textbf{Search savings and score scope $\rightarrow$} The Discussion treats causal search savings as a hypothesis requiring matched-model, matched-budget library ablations. Public-solution replay is separated from failed proposals, clone interactions, inference cost and held-out generalization. The official score is 98.1166\%, separately from 181/183 raw coverage.
\item \textbf{Formal scope and presentation $\rightarrow$} The pushout section distinguishes source persistence from behavioral validation and removes an unsupported metadata bound. The search appendix states its strong conditional success assumption. Schmidhuber's lineage is credited; auxiliary roles and subsequent designs are confined to an appendix.
\end{itemize}
