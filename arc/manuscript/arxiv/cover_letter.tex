\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the manuscript ``Pushout Attachments and Conditional
Complexity of Executable Models'' for publication in \emph{Applied
Categorical Structures}.

The paper develops a categorical framework for modular extensions of
executable models, combining pushout attachments and cofibrations in typed
presheaf categories with semantic preservation and conditional description
complexity.  We believe its connection between categorical structure and
executable-model selection is well suited to the journal.

Thank you for your consideration.
