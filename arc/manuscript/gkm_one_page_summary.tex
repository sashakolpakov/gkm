The G\"odel--Kolmogorov Machine (GKM) retains verified executable solver components
and exposes their source history for audit. The proposer contract, public Arena
interface, blank scaffold, and gate are shared across games; learned programs remain
game- and level-dependent. The intellectual lineage is Schmidhuber's G\"odel machine,
PowerPlay, and artificial curiosity.

\section*{Admission and accounting}
The host tries bounded calls to incumbent legs before requesting new code. The first
candidate reaching the exact next boundary and passing execution, independent full-path
replay, taint/provenance, and containment checks is promoted. Its full solver tree becomes
the next parent. Source cost and
\[
 F_{\rm diag}(k)=-k+0.02\sum_{j\le k}C_j^{\rm recorded}
\]
are recorded afterwards; they do not admit, reject, or rank candidates. Free energy
ranking and curiosity control are proposed extensions. This contract and later
endpoint certification do not establish that older discovery used the later containment
controls. The later no-debrief setting is distinct from inherited paid-debrief histories.
Pushouts and structure functions supply a specification and accounting background.

\section*{Source evidence and corrections}
The source proxy counts nonblank, noncomment lines plus AST container-literal elements
in \texttt{legs.py} and \texttt{players.py}. For \texttt{dc22}, the recorded workspace-local
charge totals 650. On authenticated L2--L6 pairs, $C$ sums to 601 and $G$ to 801,
taking retained size from 50 to 851; initial $G_1$ is excluded. The earlier manuscript conflated these baselines and its audit
used an incomplete source reconstruction. The solver, replay, and counters were valid.

The exact acquisition-source audit admits 171 checkpoints, 144 adjacent transitions,
and 141 marginal comparisons. It excludes \texttt{ft09} L2, \texttt{tr87} L1--L6,
\texttt{g50t} L3/L4, and \texttt{r11l} L3; missing source is not bridged. There are
54 static call-site cases referencing unchanged earlier legs, including 13 with
half-or-more conditional novelty drops. Runtime call execution is unmeasured.

\textbf{\texttt{ls20}.} The complete history has a conditional-AST drop from 737 to 247
compressed novelty bytes at L2, with a static call site to unchanged
\texttt{follow\_cardinal\_runs}. The line/literal ledger separately totals 760.
\textbf{\texttt{wa30}.} Its complete nine-level ledger totals 318 and oscillates without
an adjacent unchanged-leg case under the narrow test. Its replay has 597 actions.
Sawtooth shape is observed, not enforced or shown to cause performance.

The paired audit has 134 values in 24 games; 18 of 20 histories with enough
consecutive values reverse in both series. On aligned ranges, \texttt{wa30} has two
$C/G$ reversals each, \texttt{ls20} one each, and \texttt{dc22} two in $C$ versus none
in $G$. Its recorded L5 trough is a baseline effect. Missing pairs are not bridged.

\section*{Limits and next evaluation}
Actual calls need instrumentation. Causal search savings require a matched-model,
matched-budget library ablation. Historical discovery logs do not support a full token
bill. The companion \texttt{REVISION\_EVIDENCE.md} records frozen commits, snapshots,
reconciliation, and source-only reproduction; \texttt{RESPONSE\_TO\_REVIEWS.md} records
the revisions for editor review.
