{\Large\bfseries The G\"odel--Kolmogorov Machine: Verifier-Gated Self-Improving Solver Growth for ARC-AGI-3}\hfill {\normalsize Alexander Kolpakov, July 2026}

{\small Code and artifacts: \url{https://github.com/sashakolpakov/gkm} \;·\;
Docs: \url{https://sashakolpakov.github.io/gkm/} \;·\;
Manuscript: \texttt{arc/manuscript/arc\_agi3.tex}}

The G\"odel--Kolmogorov Machine (GKM) retains verified executable solver components
and exposes their source history for audit. The central question is how much new
description the next verified solution needs, given the retained program. The public Arena
interface, blank scaffold, and gate are shared across games; learned programs remain
game- and level-dependent. The intellectual lineage is Schmidhuber's G\"odel machine,
PowerPlay, and artificial curiosity.

\section*{Admission and accounting}
The host tries bounded calls to incumbent legs before requesting new code. The first
candidate reaching the exact next boundary and passing execution, independent full-path
replay, taint/provenance, and containment checks is promoted. Its full solver tree becomes
the next parent. Source cost and
\[
 F_{\rm diag}(k)=-k+0.02\sum_{j\le k}C_j^{\rm recorded}
\]
are recorded afterwards; they do not admit, reject, or rank candidates. Free energy
ranking and curiosity control are proposed extensions. This contract and later
endpoint certification do not establish that older discovery used the later containment
controls. The later no-debrief setting is distinct from inherited paid-debrief histories.
Pushouts and structure functions supply a specification and accounting background.

\section*{Conditional marginal complexity and the sawtooth}
The ideal coordinate is $K(P_k\mid P_{k-1})$, not a difference in source size.
The empirical proxy $M_k$ compresses novel normalized-AST statements after removing
literal matches to the preceding exact winning program. It is representation-dependent,
omits complete decoding metadata and is not a calibrated estimate of ideal $K$.

The exact-source audit admits 171 winning checkpoints and 141 comparable marginals:
64 decrease, including 21 half-or-more drops. Thirteen sharp drops coincide with
static calls to unchanged earlier legs; runtime call execution is unmeasured. Missing
sources are not bridged. The main figure plots three cases and the appendix all 25 games.

\textbf{\texttt{dc22}.} Conditional novelty is
$1058,604,716,885,828,1379$ bytes: two dips despite increasing promoted-source growth.
\textbf{\texttt{wa30}.} Its conditional profile has troughs at L2 and L6.
\textbf{\texttt{ls20}.} The L2 drop from 737 to 247 bytes coincides with a static
call to unchanged \texttt{follow\_cardinal\_runs}; later route data add novelty
while that leg remains unchanged. New machinery followed by economical reuse is
the proposed explanation, not a demonstrated cause of every trough.

\section*{Supporting source accounting}
Recorded $C$ compares with the working workspace before a winning cycle; $G$ compares
authenticated adjacent promoted states. For \texttt{dc22}, L2--L6 totals are $C=601$
and $G=801$, with retained size growing from 50 to 851; full recorded $C$ stays 650.
The manuscript interpretation and source reconstruction were corrected without
repairing the valid solver, counters or replay. These counters neither replace
conditional complexity nor reconstruct all failed discovery work.

\section*{Limits and next evaluation}
Actual calls need instrumentation. Causal search savings require a matched-model,
matched-budget library ablation. Historical discovery logs do not support a full token
bill. The companion \texttt{REVISION\_EVIDENCE.md} records frozen commits, snapshots,
reconciliation, and source-only reproduction; \texttt{RESPONSE\_TO\_REVIEWS.md} records
the revisions for editor review.
