# Leg-library debrief log

Recurring composition patterns and repeated novelty.

- Candidate higher-order leg: **prepare → transform → reseat → route**.
  Several players first stage a piece near its ring, transform equal pieces
  by merging (or cutting), reseat anything displaced by moving hazards, then
  route the remaining coupled mover to the last free ring. A future
  coordinator could accept the staging, transformation, repair, and routing
  legs while keeping each concrete skill defined only once.

- Candidate higher-order leg: **discover corner targets → merge a pair →
  seat the solids → consolidate autonomous bodies → repair the displaced
  solid → advance to completion**. Levels 8 and 9 share this orchestration
  even though their concrete merge routes and repair legs differ; a future
  coordinator could receive those level-specific legs as callbacks.
