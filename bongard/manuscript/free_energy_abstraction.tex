       An Auditable Representation and Replay Protocol}

\begin{abstract}
A rule that separates twelve Bongard support panels need not express the
intended visual concept.  It may exploit renderer details, generator reuse,
observer failure, or a post-hoc polarity reversal.  The central problem is
therefore not how to prove a sentence after a vision model writes it, but how
to expose the empirical pixel-to-observation boundary.  We describe a
Python-first protocol over the complete 12,000-task ShapeBongard V2 release.
Exact PNG bytes produce candidate-independent typed loop packets and,
optionally, neutral calibrated visual tags.  A one-shot proposer selects a
closed affirmative predicate whose clauses share one explicit object binding.
The rule must pass all supports and be durably frozen before query release;
predictions precede label reveal; Python then replays the result without a
model.  Evidence is four-valued, so failure or uncertainty cannot become a
negative and then be rescued by negation.  Lean is optional and removable, not
semantic authority.  The hardened v4 runner and v4 campaign, including an
explicit fixed five-task exact-unused TRAIN engineering mode, are
implemented and exercised.  That campaign produced 2/5 jointly correct tasks
and 4/10 fixed-denominator query predictions.  Its 4/4 released-query score is
a conditional diagnostic only: three tasks were support-rejected and only two
tasks reached query release.  There were zero terminal failures and zero
rerolls, and a cold durable replay made zero proposer/model calls.  A
support-only exhaustive
sweep returned separator counts $[0,0,0,1,1]$, and Codex selected the unique
separator on both expressible tasks.  The failures are therefore language and
witness gaps, not proposer or proof-engine misses.  These exact-image-unused
TRAIN tasks had historically exposed generator semantics, so this is
representation engineering, not strict DEV, official test, or an unbiased
generalization estimate.  For one exposed atomic task, four predicates in the
historical
2,520-member contact-inclusive diagnostic relational superlanguage separate
all twelve supports, but the
positive held-out is indeterminate under all four and no predicate separates
all fourteen panels.
An intervening report of zero support separators was a panel-mapping error.
A 24-task coverage pilot extracted all 336 panels but found far more
indeterminate than positive polygon and contact observations.  This paper
reports the protocol, the completed engineering result, these corrections,
and the remaining contact and soft-observer gaps.  A metadata-only audit also
finds that none of the 15 reserved DEV concepts is fully expressible in the
current two-closed-loop polygon/ratio language, so that run is stopped before
pixels or model calls rather than scored on accidental correlates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem statement}

A Bongard task presents six positive and six negative support panels and asks
the learner to apply the inferred distinction to held-out panels
\cite{bongard1970pattern}.  With so few examples, many hypotheses can agree on
the support labels.  Support separation alone does not identify the intended
concept.

The tempting pipeline is
\begin{equation}
  \text{panel}\longrightarrow\text{verbal description}
  \longrightarrow\text{formal predicate}\longrightarrow\text{proof}.
  \label{eq:tempting}
\end{equation}
It puts rigor after the difficult step.  A proof checker can validate
consequences of a premise; it cannot establish from syntax that ``bird-like
object'' or ``oblique edges'' is a true description of a PNG.  Serializing
prose as a predicate makes the premise precise without making it true.

Our operational decomposition is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{exact PNG bytes}
 &\longrightarrow \text{typed empirical observations}\\
 &\longrightarrow \text{closed positive Python predicate}\\
 &\longrightarrow \text{frozen decision and cold replay}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pipeline}
\end{equation}
The first arrow is empirical and fallible.  It must state provenance,
uncertainty, object identity, and scope.  The later arrows are deterministic.
This representation boundary is also suggested by the gap between raw-pixel
and structured-input Bongard systems \cite{nie2020bongardlogo,
vaishnav2026symbolic,depeweg2018solving,sonwane2021using}.

task identifiers but not other tasks sharing their semantic disclosure keys.
Selecting
\path{bd_asymmetric_goldfish-unbala_three_intersect_circles2_0000}
therefore disclosed the family and morphology keys required by the DEV witness
\path{bd_asymmetric_goldfish_0000}.  That unit is consumed rather than
restored.  The prospective selector now excludes the complete disclosure-token
closure: family plus morphology for \texttt{bd}, and pair plus attributes for
\texttt{hd}.  A metadata-only regression preserved every baseline-viable DEV
task without opening new pixels.

Selection is consuming.  The successor ledger is persisted, synchronized, and
cold-read before any selected PNG is opened.  Failure does not restore the
task, and no reroll is allowed.

\section{Candidate-independent observations}

Each panel is observed in isolation.  An extractor receives exact PNG bytes
but no support side, label, candidate formula, or query role.  It emits the
complete inventory of registered observations, not only facts useful to a
current guess.  Each packet binds the PNG digest and the exact algorithm and
catalog identities.

\subsection{Typed loop packets}

The deterministic packet retains every detected hole or loop and every
unordered loop pair.  Scenario-local loop identifiers bind:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item enclosed-area intervals and resolution substantiveness;
  \item stable polygon fits and side counts when available;
  \item minimum edge-axis obliqueness with integer millidegree units;
  \item source-component ownership; and
  \item conservative pair-contact evidence.
\end{itemize}
All serialized measurements are integer values with explicit units.  An
unstable polygon fit is unresolved, not false.

Packets can be attached as typed scene fragments through an all-or-nothing
additive gluing.  A malformed interface adds nothing.  Reuse preserves object
identity and avoids rediscovery, but does not waive packet verification.

\subsection{Point contact}

A positive point-contact signature requires two explicit loop owners, one
contact, four owner-labelled incident rays, both exterior gaps, cyclic-order
uncertainty, and provenance.  The extractor can certify some separations and
represent a positive signature inside its tested scope.

It cannot currently recognize thick-stroke vertex attachment in the exposed
atomic attempt discussed in Section~\ref{sec:a3-correction}.  Those cases stay
indeterminate.  Contact is therefore optional in synthesis; a failed contact
fit cannot certify absence.

\subsection{Soft visual tags}

Not every useful category has a convenient deterministic pixel formula.  The
experimental v1 tag catalog includes neutral categories such as
\texttt{gestalt.bird\_like} and \texttt{geometry.oblique\_edges}.  The
intended observer scores every neutrally named loop before a candidate exists.
The current envelope binds the complete object-by-tag inventory, integer score
intervals, prose, and caller-supplied prompt/model/protocol digests.  Those
opaque digests are content fields, not proof that Codex executed.

Prose is audit material and is never executed.  A closed tag predicate is
present only when its lower interval bound clears a frozen threshold.  Version
1 maps every low score to indeterminate and rejects any purported absence
authorization.  Its neutral object-presentation transport, Codex receipt
attestation, and calibration have not yet been implemented.  The evaluator now
requires and binds the actual frozen calibration record for every result, but
caller-declared digests do not establish a causal model execution; the envelope
is therefore not benchmark-admissible.  Deterministic geometry takes precedence
when a deterministic notion is available.

\section{Closed Python predicates}

The relational language has two explicit distinct roles.  Its current clauses
test polygon side count, directed enclosed-area ratio, edge-axis obliqueness,
and optional point contact.  Every clause is evaluated on the same ordered
object binding.  This prevents witnesses from unrelated loops from being
spliced into an apparently coherent relation.

This v3 language is intentionally closed but empirically too narrow.  It
requires two substantive closed loops and a directed area-ratio clause.  A
metadata-only audit of the rejected 15-task DEV cohort finds zero intended
concepts fully expressible; open arcs and bands, lamps, axes, balance,
transposition, and most symmetry concepts lie outside it.  Existing
component/hole, skeleton topology, curvature, and bilateral-symmetry witnesses
had not been glued into the earlier relational-only runner.  The implemented
repair is a finite Python tagged union over those direct witnesses, relational
queries, and symmetry thresholds,
with a pre-frozen exhaustive support-only expressibility oracle.  This first
union remains panel-global outside the relational branch and cannot form
cross-branch conjunctions; object-bound cross-leg gluing remains open.

The formula language contains affirmative registered atoms and conjunctions
only.  It exposes no arbitrary Python, \texttt{Not}, complement, disjunction,
polarity bit, or repair pass.  The proposer gets one attempt.  A support gate
passes only if every positive panel is present and every negative panel is
certified absent.

This restriction does not assert that negative visual concepts are
meaningless.  It prevents an accidental or reversed correlate from obtaining
credit after labels reveal which orientation works.  Similar concerns arise
throughout program induction and predicate invention
\cite{muggleton1994predicate,cropper2021ilp30,ellis2020dreamcoder}.

\section{Four-valued evidence}

Every empirical observation and predicate result has one of four
dispositions:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2cm,style=nextline]
  \item[\texttt{present}] a replayable affirmative witness exists;
  \item[\texttt{certified\_absent}] the observer has enough scoped evidence to
    rule the predicate out;
  \item[\texttt{indeterminate}] the observer cannot decide within its certified
    scope; and
  \item[\texttt{error}] extraction, transport, evaluation, or integrity
    checking failed.
\end{description}

Collapsing the last two states to Boolean false would allow negation to turn
missing capability into positive evidence.  The support gate therefore treats
them as terminal failure to separate, never as negative labels.

\section{Freeze, query, and replay}

The intended causal order is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item persist exposure before pixels;
  \item extract candidate-independent support packets and optional tags;
  \item make one support-only proposal;
  \item evaluate and retain the full support matrix;
  \item durably persist and cold-read the selected formula;
  \item release query pixels and commit a joint prediction;
  \item materialize labels only after prediction commitment; and
  \item replay packet integrity, evaluation, and decision without a model.
\end{enumerate}
No query-derived bytes exist at formula-selection time.  Replay verifies the
computation over recorded empirical observations; it does not retrospectively
prove those observations visually correct.

An adversarial audit rejected the first metadata-only 15-task plan before
execution.  It exposed an unkeyed digest over only 49 possible support-index
selections, allowing both held-out indices to be recovered for every task.
No DEV pixel or proposer call occurred under that plan.  The hardened v4
runner and v4 campaign now implement and fixture-test a fresh secret schedule
and hiding commitment, exact panel membership in the authenticated official
manifest, source identities stored as immutable plan fields, and an
exclusively locked durable claim journal.  Once a task is claimed, recovery
may record a terminal interruption but may never invoke the proposer again.
The completed five-task campaign and its cold durable replay exercised those
paths.

The same plan is rejected independently as a concept benchmark: the current
v3 language cannot express any of its 15 intended concepts in full.  Repairing
the hiding commitment would not repair that language mismatch.  No DEV pixels
or model calls were made.

Python is the canonical semantics for packets, predicates, decisions,
persistence, and replay.  Lean is not imported or required.  An optional
detached checker may consume persisted canonical JSON, but changing, removing,
or failing it cannot change an identity or result.  Formal verification is a
sidecar after the empirical boundary, not the source of visual truth.

\section{Atomic A3 correction}
\label{sec:a3-correction}

The historical atomic proposer asked:
\begin{quote}
Is a small triangle attached to a tilted quadrilateral?
\end{quote}
Its live scorer returned \texttt{present} for all six positive supports.  The
negative supports were three operational nonmatches, two \texttt{present}, and
one \texttt{indeterminate}.  Python stopped with
\texttt{NoExactSeparatorError} before formula freeze, query access,
prediction, label reveal, or score.

A later deterministic forensic reported a perfect 6-of-6-versus-6-of-6
shape/ratio separator.  That result is reproducible on the actual support
mapping.  A subsequent report of five resolved plus one indeterminate panel on
each side, and zero exact support separators, was caused by mapping held-out
positive source index 4 and held-out negative source index 5 into the support
set while omitting two resolved supports.

With the archived mapping restored, the base predicate---role 0 has three
sides, role 1 has four sides, and their directed area ratio is at most
$1/8$---gives six support \texttt{present} results and six support
\texttt{certified\_absent} results.  Exhaustive enumeration of the historical
2,520-member contact-inclusive diagnostic relational superlanguage finds four
exact support separators: area threshold
$1/12$ or $1/8$, each either without obliqueness or with role-1 minimum
obliqueness of 5 degrees.  All four omit contact.

This is post-hoc support resubstitution, not generalization evidence.  The
held-out positive and held-out negative are both \texttt{indeterminate} under
the $1/8$ base predicate, whose full seven-per-side result is therefore six
resolved plus one indeterminate on each side.  Under the two $1/12$ variants,
the negative held-out is \texttt{certified\_absent}, but the positive held-out
remains \texttt{indeterminate}.  No registered candidate separates all
fourteen panels.  Stable attachment perception and unresolved loop geometry
remain missing; negation would only conceal that fact.

The corrected v2 closed-language gate provides a stricter mechanical replay of
this conclusion.  It freezes all 65,678 proposer-reachable positive predicates
before reading the twelve authenticated supports and recovers exactly four
support separators.  All four are among the 1,260 contact-disabled relational
members: the 64,400 direct-count members and 18 bilateral symmetry members
contribute zero additional A3 separators.  The gate therefore
passes as a support-only protocol check, not as concept recovery or
generalization.  It reads no held-out, query, or official-test pixels and calls
no model.  Its canonical result has content address
\texttt{sha256:f9b6373df4dbe5d63807cf7e21be931db7ec0e9dfba106917df73d0e170a52d6}.

\section{Conclusion}

The missing theoretical layer is an honest empirical interface, not a Lean
term after prose.  Candidate-independent typed observations, optional neutral
calibrated tags, explicit same-binding predicates, four-valued evidence,
positive-only synthesis, commitment before query release, and model-free
Python replay make that interface auditable.  They do not make perception
infallible; instead, they force missing perception to remain visible as
indeterminacy.

The corrected atomic diagnostic has four exact support separators but zero
full-seven-per-side separators; point contact still misses its thick-stroke
attachment.  The coverage pilot quantifies how often the current observers are
indeterminate.  The 0/15 DEV expressibility audit shows that a frozen runner is
not sufficient when its language omits the target concepts.  The closed union
now passes the A3 exposed-support oracle mechanically, but the new direct-count
and symmetry branches add zero separators and no held-out panel was read.  This
clears an implementation gate, not the representation or generalization gap.
The five-task engineering campaign then scored 2/5 jointly and 4/10 on the
fixed query denominator.  Its three support rejections arose from brittle or
indeterminate witnesses; both successes selected a stable reflection-mismatch
witness.  The exhaustive support-only counterfactual found no registered
separator for any rejected task and one for each completed task, selected by
Codex both times.  The result points to empirical witness and language
coverage, not to proposer or proof-engine strength on this cohort.  It is
engineering evidence on semantics-reused TRAIN tasks, not an official or
unbiased benchmark.
