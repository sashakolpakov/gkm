# Bongard documentation moved

The canonical operational guide is [`README.md`](README.md). It covers the
complete official corpus, exposure ledger, four-disposition evidence model,
typed legs, closed positive predicate IR, freeze/query/reveal artifacts,
model-free replay, the Python-first PURE support-prototype baseline, its current
development falsification, and admission policy. Lean is optional and
removable; it is not part of the benchmark semantics.

This filename is retained for old links. Material that used to live here
described symbolic sequence experiments and an action-program Bongard-LOGO
adapter. Those paths remain available as historical controls, but they are not
raw-panel or official-corpus benchmark results.
