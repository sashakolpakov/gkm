Related Work and Claim Boundary
===============================

The project draws on Bongard problems and visual concept learning, inductive
logic programming and predicate invention, program induction and library
learning, minimum-description-length selection, calibrated selective
prediction, and proof-producing synthesis.  Its narrow question is how to
connect fallible visual observations to a replayable closed predicate without
confusing either support separation or formal verification with visual truth.

Representation before deduction
--------------------------------

Structured action programs and symbolic scene descriptions often make Bongard
tasks much easier than raw pixels.  That gap is evidence that representation is
central, but action programs supplied by a generator are not a valid input to
this visual benchmark.  The active path starts from exact PNG bytes.

The current representation uses candidate-independent loop objects, geometry,
ownership, conservative contact, and optional neutral visual tags.  A closed
Python predicate then composes those observations over one explicit object
binding.  Python is the canonical semantic and replay authority; Lean is an
optional removable sidecar and cannot change an identity or decision.  Python
can verify that computation.  Neither Python nor Lean can
prove that a soft phrase such as ``bird-like`` is visually correct merely
because it was serialized or type-checked.

Soft tags therefore remain operational observer evidence.  Their useful
formal structure is provenance, complete neutral inventory, integer confidence
intervals, frozen calibration, and four dispositions.  Version 1 licenses
positive presence only and is not yet production Codex-attested.

Why positive-only synthesis
---------------------------

Small Bongard support sets admit many accidental separators.  Reused generator
semantics, renderer details, threshold search, and observer failure make the
problem worse.  A post-hoc complement can turn any of those mistakes into an
apparently strong rule.

The active language consequently has affirmative registered atoms and
conjunctions only.  It has no polarity bit, negation, disjunction, complement
rescue, or reroll.  ``indeterminate`` and ``error`` remain distinct from
``certified_absent``.  This is a claim-control decision, not a claim that
negative concepts do not exist.

Limits of current evidence
--------------------------

Historical symbolic, semantic-cone, and abstraction-emergence controls do not
satisfy the current exact-PNG exposure/freeze/query/reveal protocol.  They may
test representation or library-accounting ideas, but they are not official
visual benchmark evidence.

A1 failed scorer transport, A2 was invalidated by live source mutation, and A3
failed a preregistered calibration-bin minimum.  The three atomic attempts
ended in wrapper failure, parser-contract failure, and no exact support
separator.  None reached a scored query.

Atomic attempt three does have a perfect deterministic shape/ratio separator
on its twelve supports: the historical 2,520-member contact-inclusive
diagnostic relational superlanguage contains four.  The
later 5+1-versus-5+1 and zero-support-separator report was a panel-mapping
error.  Both held-outs are
``indeterminate`` under the 1/8 formulas.  The 1/12 formulas certify the
negative held-out absent, but the positive remains
``indeterminate`` under all four; no candidate separates the full
seven-per-side matrix.  Point contact cannot yet recognize the task's
thick-stroke vertex attachment.

The corrected v2 65,678-member proposer-reachable closed-language A3 gate
recovers exactly those four separators.  All are among its 1,260
contact-disabled relational members; the 64,400 direct-count and 18 symmetry
members contribute zero.  The subsequent 24-task ablation used the broader
historical contact-inclusive diagnostic relational superlanguage and found
0/24 exact full separators, 0/168 exact 6+6 folds, and zero held-out
generalizers without invoking a proposer.

The complete corpus contains 12,000 tasks, but exact-image novelty is not
semantic independence.  Before the completed coverage pilot, 10,044
train/validation tasks remained exact-image-unseen after 156 exposures; the
pilot left 10,020 after 180, and the five-task campaign leaves 10,015 after
185.  Strict reusable DRILL capacity is zero,
and strict DEV fell from 16 to 15 ``bd`` tasks because the pilot protected
task IDs rather than semantic disclosure keys.  The pilot extracted all 336
panels but left most polygon and contact observations indeterminate.  Official
test remains sealed.  The hardened v4 runner and v4 campaign completed the
fixed five-task exact-unused TRAIN semantics-reused engineering mode: 2/5
jointly correct and 4/10 on the fixed query denominator.  Released queries
were 4/4, but that conditional diagnostic excludes three ``support_rejected``
tasks.  Two tasks completed, with zero terminal failures, zero rerolls, and a
cold durable replay making zero proposer/model calls.  This is not strict DEV,
official test, or unbiased generalization.  The strict 15-task DEV cohort
remains stopped at 0/15 intended-concept expressibility, official test remains
sealed, and no complete-corpus generalization claim is made.  Python remains
canonical and Lean optional and removable.  A post-hoc support-only sweep of
all 65,678 frozen predicates produced separator counts ``[0, 0, 0, 1, 1]``;
Codex selected the unique separator on both expressible tasks.  The immediate
deficit is observer/language coverage rather than a proposer miss; that sweep
is diagnostic, not a campaign metric.
