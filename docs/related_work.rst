Related Work and Claim Boundary
===============================

The project draws on Bongard problems and visual concept learning, inductive
logic programming and predicate invention, program induction and library
learning, minimum-description-length selection, calibrated selective
prediction, and proof-producing synthesis.  Its narrow question is how to
connect fallible visual observations to a replayable closed predicate without
confusing support separation or formal verification with visual truth.

Representation before deduction
--------------------------------

Structured action programs and symbolic scene descriptions make generated
Bongard tasks much easier than raw pixels.  That is evidence that
representation is central, but generator programs are not admissible inputs to
a visual benchmark.  The intended path starts from exact PNG bytes and records
candidate-independent geometry or calibrated observations before synthesis.

The repository now isolates two phases of a synthetic representation
comparison.  In phase 0, a
freshly fitted control pools 112 whole-image summary statistics; its paired
observer instead retains skeleton incidence and ordered paths, fits explicit
line and circular-arc spans, and retains an independently fitted set-valued
minimum-program inventory. Its target is a partial pure-raster component
normal form: each connected component must exactly equal one finite line or
annular-sector raster, or the target is unresolved. A post-fit target gate
does not select a pair, but it suppresses singleton claims when unresolved;
that set/GAP safety is policy-enforced rather than independent evidence.

Across two held-out synthetic carrier families and four D4 nuisances at fixed
stroke width 2 and scale 1000, this changes joint singleton accuracy from
189/432 to 432/432 on the same generated PNGs.  The family comparisons are
115/216 to 216/216 on ``radial`` and 74/216 to 216/216 on ``staggered``.  The
result supports the path-order hypothesis only on a deliberately easy,
disconnected, exact-component mechanistic benchmark.  It is not promoted to a
claim about official Bongard images or general connected action boundaries:
the grammar and collision search are bounded, only two carrier clusters are
held out, and hidden generator history is not a pixel-identifiable target in
general.

The prior pre-issuer-bridge record is

A second synthetic phase replaces that disconnected normal form with actual
ordered ``LL``, ``LA``, ``AL``, and ``AA`` raster contacts and two-shape
unions.  Its input gate recognizes only exact PNG bytes previously issued by
the process-local synthetic renderer.  This SHA-256-keyed check establishes
in-memory fixture provenance, not a digital signature, external-file
authentication, or official release authority.  Across 424 held-out PNGs, a
raw minimum exact-cover observer reaches 424/424 target-set accuracy and exact
reconstruction, versus 106/424 for the
same fixed-32 pooled-control design.  The layout comparisons are 216/216
versus 43/216 and 208/208 versus 63/208; the family comparisons are 212/212
versus 45/212 on ``radial`` and 212/212 versus 61/212 on ``staggered``.  A
212-pair pooled-feature assignment uses each held-out occurrence exactly once
and has both endpoints correct in 212/212 raw pairs versus 17/212 control
pairs.

This sharper mechanistic result has a different limitation.  The raw observer
and exhaustive target share a fixed primitive-mask catalog containing the
held-family geometry.  Carrier and full-D4-orbit separation prevent raster
replay across roles, but they do not turn the experiment into induction of an
unseen catalog or renderer grammar.  When every held-family-only catalog mask
is removed, 0/424 held-out PNGs have an exact cover.  All held-out target sets
are singletons, so the ambiguity-safety gate has no ambiguous held-out case.  The phase is
therefore fixed-catalog synthetic inversion evidence, not official transfer,
broad visual generalization, or campaign authority.  Its result digest is
``sha256:0e5f711a6e686cfb9c2b1ff2cde1559a06f15542846794ce44cde57e6a368aff``;
it does not overwrite the earlier frozen disconnected result.

The subsequent semantic layer addresses a different failure mode: ambiguity
must not be flattened into independent marginal counts before relational rule
evaluation.  It evaluates each complete 1- or 2-atom formula on every retained
program hypothesis, then applies whole-formula supervaluation.  Its complete
399-by-12 support matrix and typed freeze/commit path are validated on a
temporary synthetic archive.  This establishes deterministic rule-induction
and custody mechanics, not official visual transfer; the observer still
depends on the fixed synthetic catalog and is not authorized for official
panels.

This distinction is now operational rather than hypothetical.  An overbroad
search exposed the complete 4,400-task HD action-program authority before the
latest calibration barrier.  The campaign was abandoned and terminalized as a
typed custody GAP.  Official-test images and labels remain unopened, but the HD
program-semantic boundary is no longer sealed.

Python is the canonical semantic and replay authority.  Lean is an optional
removable sidecar: it may check consequences of persisted premises, but cannot
establish that a phrase such as ``bird-like`` or a typed geometric witness is
correct about the pixels.

Why positive-only synthesis
---------------------------

Small support sets admit accidental separators.  Reused generator semantics,
renderer details, threshold search, and observer failure make the problem
worse.  A post-hoc complement can turn any of those mistakes into an
apparently strong rule.

The active design consequently admits affirmative registered atoms and
conjunctions only.  It has no polarity bit, negation, disjunction, complement
rescue, or reroll.  ``indeterminate`` and ``error`` remain distinct from
``certified_absent``.  Codex ranks only formulas already verified against the
complete support table.

Semantic gaps and custody gaps
------------------------------

A deterministic support oracle may conclude that no registered formula fits
the evidence.  That is a semantic language or witness gap and must retain the
evaluated inventory and support matrix.

A custody GAP is different.  In the skeleton calibration incident no support
matrix or version space was constructed; digest and survivor fields are null,
not zero.  The GAP asserts only that the required authority boundary failed and
the branch is terminal.  Treating it as an empty version space would fabricate
scientific evidence from an operational incident.

Limits of current evidence
--------------------------

The fixed-32 skeleton observer, exact passed-fit protocol, role-free inference
custody, staged delayed-label runner, crash-safe tombstone, and model-free GAP
replay are material engineering advances.  They are not a benchmark result.

Earlier loop, atomic, five-task, and panel-soft results remain historical
diagnostics.  They established narrow support separators, representation
failures, and zero-coverage outcomes under their frozen protocols; they do not
authorize a new cohort or official-test claim.  Obsolete live soft/prompt code
has been removed and survives only as authenticated inert source bytes.
