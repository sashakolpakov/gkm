Related Work and Claim Boundary
===============================

The project draws on Bongard problems and visual concept learning, inductive
logic programming and predicate invention, program induction and library
learning, minimum-description-length selection, calibrated selective
prediction, and proof-producing synthesis.  Its narrow question is how to
connect fallible visual observations to a replayable closed predicate without
confusing support separation or formal verification with visual truth.

Representation before deduction
--------------------------------

Structured action programs and symbolic scene descriptions make generated
Bongard tasks much easier than raw pixels.  That is evidence that
representation is central, but generator programs are not admissible inputs to
a visual benchmark.  The intended path starts from exact PNG bytes and records
candidate-independent geometry or calibrated observations before synthesis.

This distinction is now operational rather than hypothetical.  An overbroad
search exposed the complete 4,400-task HD action-program authority before the
latest calibration barrier.  The campaign was abandoned and terminalized as a
typed custody GAP.  Official-test images and labels remain unopened, but the HD
program-semantic boundary is no longer sealed.

Python is the canonical semantic and replay authority.  Lean is an optional
removable sidecar: it may check consequences of persisted premises, but cannot
establish that a phrase such as ``bird-like`` or a typed geometric witness is
correct about the pixels.

Why positive-only synthesis
---------------------------

Small support sets admit accidental separators.  Reused generator semantics,
renderer details, threshold search, and observer failure make the problem
worse.  A post-hoc complement can turn any of those mistakes into an
apparently strong rule.

The active design consequently admits affirmative registered atoms and
conjunctions only.  It has no polarity bit, negation, disjunction, complement
rescue, or reroll.  ``indeterminate`` and ``error`` remain distinct from
``certified_absent``.  Codex ranks only formulas already verified against the
complete support table.

Semantic gaps and custody gaps
------------------------------

A deterministic support oracle may conclude that no registered formula fits
the evidence.  That is a semantic language or witness gap and must retain the
evaluated inventory and support matrix.

A custody GAP is different.  In the skeleton calibration incident no support
matrix or version space was constructed; digest and survivor fields are null,
not zero.  The GAP asserts only that the required authority boundary failed and
the branch is terminal.  Treating it as an empty version space would fabricate
scientific evidence from an operational incident.

Limits of current evidence
--------------------------

The fixed-32 skeleton observer, exact passed-fit protocol, role-free inference
custody, staged delayed-label runner, crash-safe tombstone, and model-free GAP
replay are material engineering advances.  They are not a benchmark result.

Earlier loop, atomic, five-task, and panel-soft results remain historical
diagnostics.  They established narrow support separators, representation
failures, and zero-coverage outcomes under their frozen protocols; they do not
authorize a new cohort or official-test claim.  Obsolete live soft/prompt code
has been removed and survives only as authenticated inert source bytes.
