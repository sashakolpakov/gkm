Related Work and Claim Boundary
===============================

The project combines ideas from Bongard problems and visual concept learning,
inductive logic programming and predicate invention, program induction and
library learning, minimum description length, selective prediction and
calibration, and proof-producing synthesis.  It does not claim that any of
those areas is new.

The narrow research question is whether a visual learner can accumulate a
typed reusable observation library under explicit novelty cost and
archive-preserving admission, while keeping labeled support separate from
unseen queries.

Two layers must not be conflated
--------------------------------

The desired visual vocabulary includes shape, topology, angle, contact, part
ownership, and soft descriptions, but the executable direct catalog currently
contains only ten count families.  It has no oblique-angle band, complete
point-contact signature, or persistent part-ownership predicate.  Soft
observations can still be wrong.  A closed predicate layer composes the
recorded observations and can be replayed mechanically.

Python alone is authoritative for predicate execution, calibration, cold
replay, benchmark decisions, and scientific artifact IDs.  Lean or another
proof checker may only consume an already-frozen artifact as a detached
optional sidecar; deleting it cannot change a result, decision, or ID.  Neither
Python nor Lean proves that a phrase such as ``bird-like object`` corresponds
to the pixels; formal verification starts after the empirical observation
boundary.

What the current evidence does not show
---------------------------------------

The earlier PURE support-prototype baseline failed development calibration.
Its coordinate-wise boxes lost feature correlation, one centroid per side did
not represent multimodal classes, its proposal space excluded cross-group
concepts, and its raster observables lacked needed semantic and relational
content.  That earlier diagnostic recorded 10 reversed outcomes among 132
executable support-panel outcomes.  It did not execute an A1 complement or
measure whether negation improved semantic accuracy.  The reversal evidence
motivates the current language's lack of a polarity flip or negation rescue.

The historical abstraction-emergence control supplied primitive atoms and
tested only whether reuse could reduce description length.  Generated
action-program, symbolic, semantic-cone, and other pre-rewrite pilots did not
satisfy the complete-corpus exposure and freeze/query/reveal protocol.  They
are not evidence of official visual benchmark performance and are preserved
at ``pre-bongard-complete-rewrite-20260805``.

A1 terminated before scoring.  All 48 proposer calls succeeded: 37 emitted
accepted soft claims, 10 were direct-only, and one was rejected by the typed
parser.  All 37 scorer calls were transport errors.  It produced zero scores,
kept labels withheld, and yielded no calibration, semantic accuracy, or
negation evidence.  Its command receipt is ``sha256:9aa247d9...40cb`` and its
terminal failure is ``sha256:a130d9e6...65b83``.  A2 was a distinct
repaired-protocol experiment, not an A1 retry, under protocol
``sha256:2d9261c7...81ca``.  A concurrent agent source
edit after freeze invalidated it before any Stage-A terminal artifact was
written.  Process output showed 48 proposer and 34 scorer launches only;
outputs were lost, labels were not revealed, no semantic inference is valid,
and the cohort may not be rerun.  The incident file digest is
``sha256:4ace426b...7ccd4``.  Stage B is unauthorized by both A1 and A2.
Official SEALED/test visual-semantic execution is hard-disabled.  Accordingly,
the project makes no current claim of complete-corpus visual-semantic
generalization.

A post-A2 dependency audit also corrected the unit of independence.  Distinct
HD pair IDs can share constituent attributes.  Enforcing constituent-level
disjointness against every complete-A2 exposure leaves exactly 24 DRILL units
(24 BD + 0 HD) and no HD DEV unit disjoint from the complete live ledger.  An
intermediate 28-unit upper bound had enforced this rule only within the new
batch.  Current DEV capacity is therefore 16 BD-only units, not the earlier
claimed 24 mixed-family units.
